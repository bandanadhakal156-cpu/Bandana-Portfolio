Comparative Assessment of Household Fuel Stacking, Useful Cooking Energy, and Household Energy Outcomes

Led a field-based study of energy consumption in 201 households in Ratnanagar and Khairahani, Chitwan, examining LPG, firewood, electricity and solar energy. Connected thermodynamics and energy-conversion efficiency to household energy practices.

Contributed to study methodology, field investigation, statistical analysis, interpretation and manuscript preparation. Used Python-based computation, data visualisation, logistic regression and sensitivity analysis.

Related undergraduate thesis: Fuel Stacking and Electricity Use in Two Semi-Urban Settlements in Nepal. No peer-reviewed publication is claimed.
