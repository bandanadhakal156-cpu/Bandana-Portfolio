Research & scientific inquiry

March – August 2026 · Undergraduate research · First author

Comparative Assessment of Household Fuel Stacking, Useful Cooking Energy, and Household Energy Outcomes

Led a field-based investigation of energy consumption in 201 households in Ratnanagar and Khairahani, Chitwan. The research examined the coexistence of LPG, firewood, electricity and solar energy, connecting thermodynamics and energy-conversion efficiency with household energy practices.

Contributed to methodology, field investigation, statistical analysis, interpretation and manuscript preparation. Used Python-based computation to analyse survey data, estimate useful cooking energy and visualise patterns. The CV also describes logistic regression and sensitivity analysis.

Related undergraduate thesis: Fuel Stacking and Electricity Use in Two Semi-Urban Settlements in Nepal. A peer-reviewed publication is not claimed here.
