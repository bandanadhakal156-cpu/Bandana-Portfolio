Featured research

Undergraduate thesis · 2026

Fuel Stacking and Electricity Use in Two Semi-Urban Settlements in Nepal

Led a household-energy study of 201 households in Chitwan, Nepal, exploring cooking fuels, fuel stacking, and electricity adoption. Used Python for descriptive statistics, logistic regression, sensitivity analysis, and numerical modelling. Estimated average useful cooking energy from LPG at 246 MJ per household per month.

Publication status: Manuscript submitted to Energy for Sustainable Development (per résumé; publication not yet confirmed).
